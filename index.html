Built for my own campus, then handed to it

Full-stack · Serverless

CabSy — shared-cab matching for one campus

Splitting an Uber or Ola to the station only works if you can find someone going the same way. CabSy is a college-only platform for exactly that: sign in with a verified campus Google account, post or find a ride, manage seats, and coordinate with the people you're sharing with. The in-app chat is rate-limited to 25 messages per person and wipes itself after 24 hours — enough to sort out a pickup, not a place to hang out.

It runs entirely serverless on Firebase Firestore — real-time listeners, composite indexes, and security rules that check authentication and authorisation as separate gates. The Next.js + TypeScript front end sits on Vercel, and the whole thing holds 400+ students on the free tier at ₹0 running cost.
